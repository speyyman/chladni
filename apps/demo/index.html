    What is modelled

    Thin-plate (Kirchhoff) theory. The square's modes come from a Rayleigh–Ritz solution with Legendre polynomials and match published values to five figures; the disc's are exact Bessel-function solutions. Steel: E = 200 GPa, ρ = 7850 kg/m³, ν = 0.3.

    The plate is driven at one point, each mode responding as a lightly damped oscillator (damping ratio 0.004, assumed). Sand is a random walk whose steps grow with the plate's local acceleration, so grains leave the moving parts and stop on the still lines. Bouncing, friction, air currents and grains falling off the edge are left out.

    Square-plate labels give the mode's symmetry: S or A about the vertical centre line, then the horizontal one, then the diagonal (left out for the pairs that share a frequency). Disc labels give nodal diameters and nodal circles.
